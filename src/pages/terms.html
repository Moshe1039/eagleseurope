These terms apply to every villa holiday arranged by [Company legal name] ("Eagle's", "we", "us"). They are part of the written confirmation we send you. They do not affect your rights as a consumer under the law that applies to you.

In short

  • A 30% deposit confirms your booking.
  • The balance is due 60 days before you arrive.
  • Each couple pays its own share, by bank transfer or credit card, through a personal payment link.
  • Nothing is paid in cash at the villa.
  • You can cancel at any time. What you owe depends on how close to arrival you cancel (see the table below).

1. Booking and deposit

We first send you a written proposal with the villa, what is included and the total price. Your booking is confirmed when we have received the deposit of 30% of the total price and have sent you our written confirmation. The price in the confirmation is the price you pay for everything listed in it.

2. The balance

The remaining 70% is due 60 days before your arrival date. If you book less than 60 days before arrival, the full price is due when you book. We will remind you before each due date.

3. Paying as a group

Each couple receives its own payment link and pays its own share, so nobody has to collect money from friends. The deposit, the balance date and the cancellation scale in these terms apply to each couple's own share. If one couple cancels, we talk to the organiser of the group first about the best way forward for everyone else.

4. How to pay

  • Bank transfer. The bank details and your payment reference are in your payment link and in your confirmation.
  • Credit card. You pay on a secure page of our payment provider. We never see or store your card details.

All payments are made in advance. We do not take cash at the villa. We will never change our bank details by email alone: if a message asks you to pay into a different account, please call us first.

5. If you cancel

You may cancel at any time by writing to hello@eagleseurope.com. The date we receive your message is the cancellation date. The amount you owe is a share of the total price, depending on how many days before arrival you cancel:

When you cancelWhat you owe
More than 90 days before arrivalThe 30% deposit (not refunded)
90 to 60 days before arrival50% of the total price
59 to 30 days before arrival75% of the total price
Fewer than 30 days before arrival, or if you do not arrive100% of the total price

What you have already paid counts towards this amount. If you have paid more than you owe, we refund the difference within 14 days of your cancellation.

6. If we have to cancel

If we cannot provide your holiday as confirmed, for example because the villa becomes unavailable, we will tell you straight away. You can choose a comparable alternative that we offer, or receive a full refund of everything you have paid. We make the refund within 14 days.

7. Unavoidable circumstances

If unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at or near the destination, such as a natural disaster or an official travel ban, significantly affect your holiday, you can cancel without a cancellation charge and we refund what you have paid.

8. Travel insurance

We strongly recommend travel insurance that covers cancellation and medical care, bought when you pay your deposit. Our cancellation scale applies even if you cannot travel because of illness or other personal reasons, and insurance is how most guests protect themselves against that.

9. Changing your booking

If you want to change dates, guests or other details, tell us as early as possible. We will tell you what is possible, and any extra cost, before you agree to anything.
