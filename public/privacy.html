1. Who we are

For any question about your data, write to privacy@eagleseurope.com. [If the company is established outside the EU: Our representative in the European Union under Article 27 GDPR is [name, address, email].]

We apply the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) to all personal data we handle, wherever you live. [If the company is registered in Israel: We also comply with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981.]

2. What data we collect

When you send an enquiry

Your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number; whether you would like information or a call; the size of your group, the destination and dates you have in mind; anything you write in the message field; and the date and wording of the consent you gave.

When your group books a trip

We send each couple a personal link to a secure form. Through it we collect, for each guest: full name as shown on the passport, date of birth, nationality, passport number and expiry date, and any allergies or dietary needs. For each couple we also collect flight numbers and times, any celebrations during the trip, wishes for the kitchen and any other notes you choose to share. The organiser of the group may give us the names, email addresses and phone numbers of the other couples so we can send them their link.

When we communicate

The content of emails, WhatsApp messages and calls with us, and the preferences you tell us about along the way.

Payments

Records of what each couple has paid and when. Card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored by us.

We do not collect more than we need to plan your trip, and we never buy personal data from third parties.

4. Allergies and dietary needs

Information about allergies or medical dietary needs is health data, a special category under the GDPR. We ask for it only so that your chef can cook safely for you, we collect it only with your explicit consent on the guest form, and we share it only with the people preparing your food. You can leave the field empty, but we then cannot take allergies into account.

5. Who we share your data with

Only the people who need it to deliver your trip, and only the part they need:

  • Villa owners and property managers: guest names and, where required by local law, passport details for guest registration.
  • Your private chef: names, allergies, dietary needs, celebrations and kitchen wishes.
  • Drivers and hosts: names, phone numbers and flight details.
  • Experience providers (wineries, boat trips, guides): names and group size.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it (for example guest registration in the destination country) or to answer a lawful request.
  • Our service providers, who process data on our behalf under a data processing agreement: Cloudflare (website hosting and database), our email provider, WhatsApp (Meta) for messages you choose to exchange with us there, our payment provider, and our accountants and legal advisers.

If our business is ever sold or merged, your data may pass to the new owner, who must keep honouring this policy.

6. International transfers

Your trip takes place in the European Union or Switzerland, and most of the people we share data with are located there. Some of our service providers, such as Cloudflare and Meta, are based in the United States; where they handle personal data from the European Economic Area, they rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. [If the company or team is based in Israel: Israel is recognised by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection.]

7. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: deleted 24 months after our last contact with you.
  • Passport details, dates of birth, flights, allergies and other guest details: deleted within 30 days after the end of your trip, unless the law of the destination country requires us to keep them longer.
  • Booking and payment records: kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

You can ask us to delete your data earlier at any time (see section 10).

8. How we protect your data

  • All traffic to our website is encrypted (HTTPS), and the database is encrypted at rest.
  • Passport numbers and dates of birth are additionally encrypted with a separate key before they are stored.
  • Each couple's form is reached through a private, randomly generated link that search engines are told not to index, and that stops working once the trip data has been deleted.
  • Only our team can see guest data, through a password-protected admin area; passport numbers are masked on screen.

No system is completely secure. If a breach ever puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the competent authority as the law requires.

9. Cookies and website data

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, so there is no cookie banner. Like every website, our hosting provider Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against attacks. Some photographs and films on the site are currently loaded from Unsplash, Pexels and Mixkit, which means those services also receive your IP address when you view them. If we ever add analytics or cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it.

Write to privacy@eagleseurope.com. We reply within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work, or where you think a breach happened. [If the company is registered in Israel: or to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.] We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

11. Data about other people in your group

If you give us details of other travellers, such as the other couples in your group or children travelling with you, please let them know and point them to this policy. Each couple fills in their own details through their personal link whenever possible. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children; details of guests under 18 are provided by their parents or guardians.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our service develops. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you directly about any important change that affects a trip you have booked.
