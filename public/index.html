Our vision

We don't rent villas.
We host journeys.

Every group of friends has one couple who organizes everything: the dates, the house, the endless message threads, the chasing. They come home needing a holiday of their own.

Eagle's exists so that nobody in the group has that job. We choose the house, bring in the chef, meet you at the airport and quietly look after every detail, including a few you won't see coming.

Questions

answered.

What is an all-inclusive villa holiday with Eagle's?

A private villa for your group with everything arranged around it: a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, a stocked kitchen, private airport transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region. You agree one price before you travel, so nobody splits bills at the table.

Can we book a villa with a private chef in Europe?

Yes. Every Eagle's journey includes a private chef who cooks in the villa from local produce. We arrange villas with a chef across Italy, Greece, France, Spain, Portugal, Croatia, Switzerland, Austria and Germany, from Tuscany and the Amalfi Coast to Crete, Mallorca and the Algarve.

Is a catered villa a good idea for a group of friends?

It is the easiest way to holiday together. Everyone has a private bedroom and bathroom, meals happen around one long table without anyone cooking or cleaning, and the days stay flexible. It suits groups of couples celebrating together, and families travelling with friends.

How many couples can stay in one villa?

Most of our groups are between two and seven couples. Tell us how many you are and we will match you to a villa with a private bathroom for every couple and enough space to spend time together and apart.

Do you plan villa holidays for birthdays and anniversaries?

Often. A 40th or 50th birthday, an anniversary or simply a reunion: tell us what you are celebrating and we will shape the menu, the setting and a few surprises around it.

How does payment work when several couples travel together?

Each couple pays their own share directly to us, with a personal payment link. Nobody has to collect money from friends, and we gather passports, flights and dietary needs from each couple too.

When is the best time for a villa holiday in Europe?

Late spring (May and June) and early autumn (September and October) are ideal for the Mediterranean: warm sea, fewer crowds and the best produce. The Alps are wonderful for lakes and walking in summer, and for chalets in winter.

What is the difference between a staffed villa and a holiday rental?

With a holiday rental you get the keys and organise everything yourselves. With a staffed villa from Eagle's the house comes with a chef, housekeeping, transfers and a host, and someone has already thought through the details before you arrive.
