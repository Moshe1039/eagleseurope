Regions

Regions in Spain

  • Mallorca villas with a private chef

    Fincas in the Tramuntana mountains, coves on the east coast and the old town of Palma. Catered villas in Mallorca are a favourite for groups.

  • Ibiza villas with a private chef

    The quieter, beautiful side of the island: villas in the north, sunset terraces and beach lunches.

  • Andalusia villas with a private chef

    Cortijos among olive trees, white villages, Seville and Granada within reach, and the coast near Marbella.

  • Costa Brava villas with a private chef

    Rocky coves, medieval villages and Catalan cooking, an easy distance from Barcelona.

When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    May, June, September and October are warm without the peak-summer heat. The islands stay pleasant well into October.

  • At the table

    Tapas to start, rice cooked over fire, fish from the morning's catch and Spanish wines from Rioja to the islands.

  • Included

    The villa, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.
