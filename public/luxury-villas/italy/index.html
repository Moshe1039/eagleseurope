Regions

Regions in Italy

  • Tuscany villas with a private chef

    Cypress roads, Chianti and Montepulciano wineries, hill towns like Montalcino and Cortona. A Tuscany villa with a chef is our most requested journey. A group can mix its own days: a Chianti estate wine tasting, a morning cooking class with the villa's own chef, or an afternoon wandering a hilltop town's piazza before dinner back at the villa.

  • Amalfi Coast villas with a private chef

    Lemon terraces and cliffside villas above the sea, with boat days to Capri and long dinners as the lights come on in Positano.

  • Puglia villas with a private chef

    Whitewashed masserie among centuries-old olive groves, trulli in the Itria Valley and an unhurried coastline, ideal for larger groups. Evenings can mean a long dinner under the stars in a masseria courtyard, or an afternoon among the olive trees tasting Salento's wines and cheeses.

  • Sicily villas with a private chef

    Baroque towns, Etna wines and a cuisine of its own. Staffed villas near Noto, Taormina and the west coast.

  • Lake Como villas with a private chef

    Grand lakeside villas, gardens running down to the water and evenings on the terrace with the mountains opposite.

  • Sardinia villas with a private chef

    Some of Europe's clearest water, quiet coves and villas tucked into the macchia above the sea.

When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    May, June, September and October are ideal: warm days, the sea already swimmable in the south and the harvest season in Tuscany. July and August are hot and busy, best for the lakes and the coast.

  • At the table

    Fresh pasta made by hand, local olive oil, seasonal vegetables from the market and wines from the valley you are staying in.

  • Included

    The villa, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.

Questions

Villas in Italy,
answered.

Can we rent a villa in Tuscany with a private chef?

Yes. Every Eagle's villa in Tuscany includes a private chef for breakfast and dinner, plus housekeeping, transfers and a host. One evening can become a hands-on pasta workshop with the chef.

Which region of Italy is best for a group of friends?

Tuscany and Puglia have the most spacious villas for groups of several couples. The Amalfi Coast and Lake Como suit smaller groups who want a view and a sense of occasion.

Can one couple organise a multi-couple trip to Puglia without chasing everyone for money?

Yes. Eagle's Europe runs on one all-inclusive price per person for the whole group, so there is no line-item splitting between couples. Each couple pays its own share directly, a deposit to confirm and the balance before arrival, so the organising couple never has to collect money from friends.

How do we organise a multi-couple trip to Tuscany without arguing over who gets which bedroom?

Easily: every Eagle's villa is one all-inclusive price per person, not a price list per room, so there is no haggling over who pays more for the better bedroom. Each couple pays the same share, with a deposit to confirm and the balance due before arrival, so room assignment never turns into a negotiation.
