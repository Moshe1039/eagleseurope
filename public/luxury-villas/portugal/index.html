Regions

Regions in Portugal

  • Algarve villas with a private chef

    Golden cliffs, hidden coves and villas with pools a short drive from the beach. Beyond the sand, a group can fill its own days: a boat trip along the sea caves, an afternoon in Tavira's old town, or a cliff walk to the next cove before everyone's back at the villa for dinner.

  • Comporta villas with a private chef

    Rice fields, dunes and endless beaches south of Lisbon, with a barefoot, design-led style.

  • Douro Valley villas with a private chef

    Terraced vineyards along the river, port and wine quintas, and boat trips through the valley.

  • Sintra villas with a private chef

    Palaces and forested hills near Lisbon, with the Atlantic coast close by.

Plan your trip

When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    April to June and September to October are ideal. The Algarve stays mild late into autumn.

  • At the table

    Grilled fish and seafood, cataplana, local cheeses and wines from the Douro and Alentejo.

  • Included

    The villa, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.

Questions

Villas in Portugal,
answered.

Can we stay in a quinta in the Douro Valley?

Yes. We arrange wine estates and villas in the Douro with a private chef and private tastings.

Is the Algarve good outside summer?

Yes. Spring and autumn are warm and calm, with far fewer visitors.

Is there an all-inclusive option in the Algarve that isn't a resort hotel?

Yes. Every Eagle's villa in the Algarve comes with a private chef, housekeeping, private transfers and a host built into one all-inclusive price per person, so you get the privacy of your own villa with nothing left to arrange yourselves, rather than a self-catering villa where a chef or transfers are extras booked separately.
