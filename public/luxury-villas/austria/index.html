When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    June to September for lakes and walking; December to March for skiing.

  • At the table

    Alpine cooking with a lighter touch: lake fish, mountain cheeses, Austrian pastries and wines from the Wachau.

  • Included

    The villa, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.
