When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    June brings the lavender in Provence. May, June and September are best on the Riviera, and September and October are harvest time in Bordeaux.

  • At the table

    Market vegetables, cheeses from the region, fresh fish on the coast and wines poured from the vineyards around you.

  • Included

    The exclusive private resort, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.
