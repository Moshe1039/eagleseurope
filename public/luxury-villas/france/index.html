Regions

Regions in France

  • Provence villas with a private chef

    Lavender fields in early summer, hilltop villages in the Luberon and market mornings in towns like L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue.

  • Côte d'Azur villas with a private chef

    Villas above the Mediterranean between Saint-Tropez and Menton, with beach clubs, boat days and the old towns of the Riviera.

  • Dordogne villas with a private chef

    Châteaux above the river, quiet countryside, truffles and walnuts: a slower, greener France.

  • Bordeaux wine country villas with a private chef

    Vineyard estates around Saint-Émilion and the Médoc, with private tastings arranged at the châteaux.

When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    June brings the lavender in Provence. May, June and September are best on the Riviera, and September and October are harvest time in Bordeaux.

  • At the table

    Market vegetables, cheeses from the region, fresh fish on the coast and wines poured from the vineyards around you.

  • Included

    The villa, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.
