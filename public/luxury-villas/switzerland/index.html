Regions

Regions in Switzerland

  • Engadin & St. Moritz villas with a private chef

    High alpine lakes, larch forests and one of the world's original winter resorts.

  • Zermatt villas with a private chef

    Car-free village life beneath the Matterhorn, with chalets for summer walking and winter skiing.

  • Lavaux & Lake Geneva villas with a private chef

When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    June to September for lakes and walking; December to April for skiing and catered chalets.

  • At the table

    Alpine cheeses, fondue and raclette nights, lake fish and Swiss wines rarely found outside the country.

  • Included

    The villa, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.

Very. Lakeside villas, mountain walks and mild weather make it a cool alternative to the Mediterranean in July and August.
