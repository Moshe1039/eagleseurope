Regions

Regions in Greece

  • Crete villas with a private chef

    Greece's largest island: mountain villages, gorges, lagoons like Balos and villas above Chania and Elounda.

  • The Cyclades villas with a private chef

    Mykonos, Paros, Naxos and Antiparos: white villages, windmills and boat days between islands.

  • Peloponnese villas with a private chef

    Quiet bays, olive groves and ancient sites, with villas near Porto Heli and the Mani.

  • Corfu villas with a private chef

    Green hills running down to the Ionian Sea, Venetian old town and calm swimming coves.

When to go, and what's on the table

  • Best time to go

    May, June, September and October are best: warm sea, fewer crowds and softer heat. July and August are hot and the islands are busiest.

  • At the table

    Greek salads with island tomatoes, grilled fish, slow-cooked lamb, local cheeses, olive oil and wines from Crete and Santorini.

  • Included

    The villa, a private chef for breakfast and dinner, daily housekeeping, private transfers, a host on call and experiences in the region.

Can we book a villa in Crete with a private chef?

Yes. Our Crete villas include a private chef, housekeeping, transfers and a host, with days out to villages, wineries and beaches.

Which Greek island is best for a group villa holiday?

Crete offers the most variety and space. Paros and Antiparos suit groups who want island life without the Mykonos crowds.
