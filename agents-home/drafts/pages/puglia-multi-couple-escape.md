# A Puglia Escape for Your Whole Group — Without the Group-Chat Logistics

You already know the shape of this trip: a few couples, one of you doing most of the organizing, and a group chat that's been "we should really do this" for over a year. Puglia is the part everyone agrees on. What usually falls apart is everything after that — whose villa, whose dates, who's paying for what, and who ends up chasing six different quotes from six different people.

Eagle's Europe handles that part. One all-inclusive package, one price per person, and a single point of contact — yours — instead of a dozen loose threads to hold together.

## What "All-Inclusive" Actually Means Here

This isn't a hotel package dressed up in villa photos. It's a single curated arrangement built around your group, covering the villa stay and the experiences around it, so the organizing couple isn't the one stitching together a private chef here and a boat trip there.

As part of your package, your group picks **two of the following six experiences** in and around Puglia:

- A dinner at a working masseria, under the stars, built around the region's own table
- A guided tasting of Salento's wines and cheeses
- A walk through the centuries-old olive groves that define this stretch of coastline
- A day tracing the Ostuni–Alberobello–Polignano a Mare route, at a pace that isn't a bus-tour pace
- A boat day along the Adriatic coastline

## Built for a Group That's Already Decided to Go Together

This is for the group that's past "should we go" and stuck on "how do we actually make this happen" — typically several couples in their 40s and up, with one couple carrying the planning. You don't need to find the villa, negotiate with six different suppliers, or become the person your friends are quietly annoyed at by the time you land.

Each couple pays its own share. There's a deposit to secure the dates, with the balance due well ahead of arrival — no surprises, no one couple fronting the group's money.

## Why Puglia, Specifically

Puglia rewards a group that wants more than a villa with a pool. Whitewashed hill towns, masserie that have been doing this longer than any of us have been alive, olive groves that aren't a backdrop so much as the whole point, and a coastline that doesn't need a resort built on top of it to be worth the trip. It's a region built for slow mornings and long dinners with the people you came with — which is the actual point of a multi-couple trip in the first place.
