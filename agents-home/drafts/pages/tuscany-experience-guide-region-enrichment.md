## Why this entry, and why this framing

The topic-map entry's research found that competitor villa listings in Chianti/Lucca (Colli,
Casa Matteucci) each sell a single location's amenities but never frame the region as a menu of
experiences a group picks from — which maps directly onto this product's own "pick 2 of 6
attractions" mechanic. The existing Tuscany copy already named the wine region (Chianti,
Montepulciano) and the hill towns (Montalcino, Cortona) but never connected them to anything a
group would actually *do*, and never mentioned the cooking class that's already implied
elsewhere on the page (every Italy villa comes with a private chef, per the page's own intro).

The added sentence names three concrete, pickable days — wine tasting, cooking class, hilltop
town — using the villa's chef (already an established fact on the page) rather than inventing a
new service, and echoes the product's own pick-your-experiences structure without stating a
price or a specific attraction count.

## What this is not

- Not a new page or route — Tuscany already has a region entry on Italy's page; this is a region

- Not villa-specific content — no individual villa's photos, price, or description used; no

- Not a duplicate — the two existing Tuscany-related FAQ entries on Italy's page (chef
  availability, bedroom/cost-splitting) address different questions (service availability,
