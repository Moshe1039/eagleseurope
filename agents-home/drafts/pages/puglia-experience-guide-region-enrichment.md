## Why this entry, and why this framing

The topic-map entry's research found specific, recurring experience phrasing in search
results — "dinner under the stars in a masseria," "tasting of typical wines and cheeses," "a day
among the centuries-old olive trees of Salento" — scattered across low-authority travel-blog and
agency sources, with no single authoritative destination guide consolidating them. Since the
site has no "experience guide" page type (only `/` and `/luxury-villas/<country>/` exist), the
suggested page type is corrected here the same way the four prior topic-map entries were: the
real research signal (specific named experiences a group could use to decide what to do) is
folded into the existing Puglia region description instead of a new page.

The added sentence names the three specific experiences the research flagged (masseria dinner,
olive groves, wine/cheese tasting) using phrasing close to what real searches use, without
inventing anything not already true of the destination or already implied by the surrounding
copy (the existing sentence already said "masserie among olive groves" and "Itria Valley" — this
adds the specific activities a group would actually do there).

## What this is not

- Not a new page or route — Puglia already has a region entry on Italy's page; this is a region
  description enrichment only, exactly the kind of "existing region/intro string" edit the
  content-writer is scoped to.
- Not villa-specific content — no individual villa's photos, price, or description used, and no
  named competitor or source appears in the live copy, only in this internal research record.
- Not a price — no number is stated anywhere, and no claim about meal pricing or what's included
  beyond what's already established elsewhere on Italy's page (private chef, host, transfers).
- Not a duplicate — this is the first mention of a masseria dinner or Salento wine/cheese tasting
  anywhere in Italy's copy; the existing Puglia FAQ entries (bedroom/cost-splitting) address
  group-organizing pain points, not destination experiences, so there's no overlap.
