# Amalfi Coast region enrichment

## What changed

Appended two sentences to the existing Amalfi Coast region description in Italy's `regions`
tuple (`src/countries.py`), naming a specific day: a group based in Ravello or a quieter town
like Conca dei Marini walking the Path of the Gods trail from close to their own door, finishing
at Villa Cimbrone's cliff gardens, with the evening left free for Positano.

## Why (research grounding)

Matches `research/topic-map.md`'s "Amalfi Coast: A Day on the Path of the Gods, Ending in
Ravello" entry (added 2026-11-19 by the keyword-researcher fallback pass). WebSearch showed
guided-tour sellers (AAA TripCanvas's "Private Guided Tour to the Path of the Gods – Half Day,"
The Roman Guy's "Positano, Amalfi and Ravello Day Trip from Naples") selling this exact day as a
day trip from Naples, while villa-rental guides (ItalyPlanner's "Best Villas to Rent on the
Amalfi Coast") recommend basing in Ravello, Conca dei Marini or Praiano for easy trail access but
never connect that to Villa Cimbrone's gardens as part of the same day. The site's prior
Amalfi Coast copy named neither the trail, Ravello, nor the gardens — only "lemon terraces,"
"boat days to Capri" and "dinners... in Positano."
