## Why this entry, and why this framing

The topic-map entry's research found that competitor content for the Algarve is almost entirely
hotel- or beach-focused, and the group-villa results that do exist stop at "the villa has a pool
and sea views" — none go beyond that into a structured set of inland/cultural options. The
existing Algarve copy already named the geography (cliffs, coves) but never connected it to
anything a group would actually *do* beyond lying by the pool, and never acknowledged Tavira or
any other old-town option, despite the research specifically flagging historic Tavira and boat
trips as the named, underserved angle.

The added sentence names three concrete, pickable days — a sea-cave boat trip, an old-town
afternoon in Tavira, a cliff walk — none of which are a new company service: boat trips are
already an established activity type on this same Portugal page (the Douro Valley region
description already says "boat trips through the valley"), and a cliff walk or an old-town visit
are just descriptive geography, not a paid add-on. This echoes the product's own pick-your-
experiences structure (the same framing used for the Puglia and Tuscany region enrichments)
without stating a price, a specific attraction count, or any new unverifiable claim.

## What this is not

- Not a new page or route — the Algarve already has a region entry on Portugal's page; this is a
  region description enrichment only.
- Not villa-specific content — no individual villa's photos, price, or description used; no
  named competitor appears in the live copy, only in this internal research record.
- Not a price — no number stated, and the boat trip mirrors language already live elsewhere on
  this same page (Douro Valley), not a new paid service invented for this edit.
- Not a duplicate — the two existing Algarve-related FAQ entries on Portugal's page ("Is the
  Algarve good outside summer?" and the all-inclusive-vs-resort FAQ) address different questions
  (seasonality, service structure) than this destination-experience sentence.
