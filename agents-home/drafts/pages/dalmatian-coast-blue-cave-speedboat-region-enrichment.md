# Dalmatian Coast region enrichment

## What changed

Appended one sentence to Croatia's existing "Dalmatian Coast" region description in
`src/countries.py`, naming a specific speedboat day: a crossing from Split through the Blue
Cave on Biševo, a stop in Hvar town, and swim breaks around the Pakleni Islands.

Before:

> Split and Dubrovnik, stone exclusive private resorts by the water and islands a short boat
> ride away.

After:

> Split and Dubrovnik, stone exclusive private resorts by the water and islands a short boat
> ride away. A group can fill its own day with a speedboat out from Split through the Blue Cave
> on Biševo, a stop in Hvar town, and swim breaks around the Pakleni Islands, back in time for
> the evening light on the water.

## Why

Per the topic-map entry: multiple independent operators out of Split (musement.com, manawa.com's
"Blue Cave, Hvar & 6 islands" tour, clicatour.com, icelolly.com) sell this exact named full-day
speedboat itinerary — Blue Cave, Hvar town, Pakleni Islands — but always as a standalone day tour
from Split or Trogir, never bundled with a villa stay. Separately, villa-rental listings for the
Split area (A.M.A Selections, Villaway, Oliver's Travels, Villas of Distinction) describe bedroom
count, pool and sea view only, naming none of these specific sites. Croatia's existing Dalmatian
Coast copy named no island, cave or itinerary — the same generic-description gap already closed
for Hvar and Istria elsewhere on this same Croatia page. No claim of search volume is made,
consistent with `context/rules.md`.

## Notes

- No price mentioned anywhere, per the house rule.
- No villa-specific content (image/price/description) used — this only names a public natural
  sight (Biševo's Blue Cave) and public destinations (Hvar town, Pakleni Islands), not any
  specific rental property or owner.
- Matches house voice/style of the neighboring Hvar and Istria entries on this same Croatia page
  — short, warm, specific, naming the group's own day rather than villa amenities.
