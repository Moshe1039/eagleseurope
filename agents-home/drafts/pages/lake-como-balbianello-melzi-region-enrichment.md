# Lake Como region enrichment

## What changed

Appended one sentence to Italy's existing "Lake Como" region description in `src/countries.py`,
naming a specific boat-and-tasting day: a crossing from Bellagio to Villa del Balbianello and the
gardens of Villa Melzi, finishing with a wine tasting at a lakeside enoteca, offered as an
alternative to a quieter afternoon on the terrace.

Before:

> Grand lakeside exclusive private resorts, gardens running down to the water and evenings on
> the terrace with the mountains opposite.

After:

> Grand lakeside exclusive private resorts, gardens running down to the water and evenings on
> the terrace with the mountains opposite. A group can fill its own day with a boat out from
> Bellagio to Villa del Balbianello and the gardens of Villa Melzi, finishing with a wine tasting
> at a lakeside enoteca, or a quieter afternoon back on the terrace with the mountains opposite.

## Why

Per the topic-map entry: multiple independent boat-tour operators out of Bellagio
(lakecomotravel.com's "Three Villas" tour, byfood.com, lokalee.app's wine-tasting boat tours)
sell this exact named itinerary — Villa del Balbianello, the gardens of Villa Melzi, a Bellagio
wine tasting — but only ever as a standalone boat excursion, never bundled with a stay. Separately,
villa-rental listings for the same stretch of shore (Marriott Homes & Villas, Engel & Völkers,
Plum Guide, Villas of Distinction) describe bedroom count, pool and lake view only, naming none
of these places. Italy's existing Lake Como copy was generic with no named place — the same gap
already closed for Tuscany, Puglia, Sardinia and Sicily elsewhere on the Italy page. No claim of
search volume is made, consistent with `context/rules.md`.

## Notes

- No price mentioned anywhere, per the house rule.
- No villa-specific content (image/price/description) used — this only names public

  enoteca," not any specific rental property or owner.
- Matches house voice/style of neighboring Italy region entries (Sardinia, Sicily) — short,
  warm, specific, offering a choice between the named activity and a quieter alternative.
